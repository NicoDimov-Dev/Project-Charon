# The Perfect Assassin — a short tale

*Retold from the game's own script (PASSTEXT): its dialogue, console entries and objectives. Names and events follow the 1997 text.*

---

## I. The contract

Charon is a blue-skinned killer who no longer remembers who he is. In the dark of his ship, a voice comes over the comm:

*"Charon! I have a contract for you, assassin. I know you have lost your memory, but you are still the Perfect Assassin and we need your services. I am Ul D-Grak. Seek me at the Carteele on the planet Kar-Naq and I shall give you the details."*

So he goes down to Kar-Naq, a desert world whose crops are sand and kirith. Kirith is a hallucinogen grown from a carnivorous fungus, and half the galaxy is addicted to it.

## II. The canyon

He lands in a red canyon outside the walled city of **Kar-Es-Taal**. The dead are already there. Pra'min Kommandoes have been through: crustacean mercenaries whose shells are sculpted to suit their jobs. The **komms pra'min** have shells carved into satellite dishes, so they can call their orbiting mothership. Every pra'min carries a Cho-Lok suicide device, an anti-matter bomb for a lost cause.

In the dirt lies **G'sset**, a Jakhari worker. The pra'min tortured him for hours.

*"Help me! I'm in terrible pain… Please kill me before they return."*

Charon gives him the mercy of the gun.

Behind a rock crouches **Ich'Flah**, a doolan official. He is small, robed and blue, and very pleased with his own importance.

*"Keep down or the pra'min will get you! I was supervising the jakhari hiding ammo in the caves when they attacked. I've got a gun, but I'm saving it to protect myself."*

The Doolan Doorman bars the city gates. Entry needs a citizen's identity number, and the komms pra'min stole half of the doolan's code. Ich'Flah strikes a bargain: return the **porta-spa** he dropped while running away, a little sprayer that keeps doolan skin from cracking in the sun. In exchange, he'll key in his own forged code. In the smugglers' caves, where the Jakhari had been hiding ammunition, Charon finds the spray. One cave is sealed and could be blasted open. Ich'Flah keys in the code, and the gates of Kar-Es-Taal open.

## III. The city that turns

Kar-Es-Taal sits on the planet's northern axis and **rotates with the sun**. One side is always in full sun: the Golden Crescent. The far side is perpetual night: the Shades. Between them is twilight: the Cusp.

The Jakhari built it all. They are a slow, hardy, ancient race, and now they do the city's labour. The doolan "do the thinking", carry the nasty guns, and pray to the Pontifex Gulam Shu XXIII: *"your life is commerce, commerce is your life."* The Militia keeps order, and under the city lies **the Hub**. The Hub used to be the machinery that turns the city; now it is a self-run prison.

Nobody has heard of Ul D-Grak, and nobody admits to knowing what the Carteele is. Everyone says the same thing: *ask Dah'Mon Targ.*

## IV. The Mojo Ladder

Dah'Mon Targ owns the **Mojo Ladder**, the only licensed nightclub in the city. It is the birthplace of the Jakhari Jive and the Doolan Dip. Getting in takes a membership card, and membership is "closed".

So Charon works the streets.
- A forger, **In'Khee**, will doctor a card if Charon brings him someone else's card and a photo-booth picture. The picture costs a coin, and the coin comes from the fountain in Market Square.
- A sideshow Jakhari gives away a teddy bear to anyone who shoots all his ducks. The bear turns out to be stuffed with kirith.
- Muggers, lost tickets and lovesick doormen fill in the rest.

Inside, Dah'Mon wants a favour first: two Jakhari thugs are coming to shake him down. Charon deals with them.

*"Nice job, Blue. So that gun's not just for show."*

When Charon asks again, the fish-faced owner tells him the truth. The **Carteele** is an illegal, televised death-match held in the Hub, where convicts fight robots for a few moments of glory. Only **Karanni L'Bajo Maccam** knows the way in, and knows who Ul D-Grak is.

## V. Karanni, the Blimp

Karanni is the fattest doolan in the city and its kirith kingpin. He lives in a mansion, permanently submerged in a vat of the drug. Only his "special friends" are allowed in, so Charon follows **Smoo-Chee**, a Jakhari "authorised visitor", through the gates.

*"So, Charon, my people tell me you seek information; dangerous information."*

Karanni has the answers, but they have a price. A young upstart, **Akim Quorta Khan**, is moving in on his empire. Akim is an arms smuggler and the rumoured killer of the planet's governor, hiding somewhere in the Shades. *"Bring me his head. That would be rather amusing."*

Charon goes through the demolition district and a Jakhari who won't let anyone through his house until his missing **space-slug** comes home. He blows open a trapdoor and finds Akim barricaded in his lair, offering a partnership. Charon answers: *"Thanks for the offer, but I've been hired to kill you and I'm a professional."*

He drops the head in front of Karanni. Karanni suddenly remembers nothing: *"I can't seem to remember anything about the Carteele or Ul D-Grak. Sorry. Close the door behind you."*

Karanni's right-hand man, **Kol'Shee**, has ambitions of his own and whispers a better plan: clip a **neurotoxin** canister onto the valve of the kirith vat. Charon does it. With the poison seeping in, Karanni talks: *"She's in the Arena waiting for you… you have to survive the Carteele. Midima K-Kuhl runs the show."*

Ul D-Grak is a *she*.

## VI. The Hub and the Carteele

Getting into the Hub takes:
- a doolan's bachelor beads, Karanni's beads, as the "trophy" demanded by the Hub boss **Midima K-Kuhl**;
- a rusty hatch code from a grateful freedom fighter;
- baked rats, because the prisoners like their rats. In the Jakhari Holy Book only black rats are clean.

**The Carteele.** It is broadcast live to the whole city. There are four zones, each with a hidden key, and the doors lock behind him. Armed guard robots wait in every zone, "very enthusiastic". No one has survived in three years. Charon does.

In the winner's enclosure she is waiting. *"I'm Ul D-Grak. You've been looking for me. My real name is K L'Hanna."*

She looks like Charon's own species. She promises to give him back his memory, and the target sits somewhere that can only be reached aboard a **Time Slider**, a creature that swims between dimensions. The Militia keeps one locked in Hangar 18.

## VII. The Militia Headquarters

The Headquarters stand over the Hub, ringed by the canals that turn the city.
- **The resistance:** Charon joins the Jakhari resistance, whose memos travel by D-Mail, "duck mail", flown by a doolan in an aviary.
- **The password:** "T-Chktgrk", which he can't pronounce because he hasn't got the tentacles.
- **The way in:** through flooded maintenance tunnels, then the security doors, and finally the outer gate, which he opens for K L'Hanna.

Along the way he meets the strangest enemy yet: **himself**.

*"I'm you! But I'm a bigger, better, tougher, nastier version from your future… You can't be allowed to fulfil your contract. You don't know what will happen if you do."*

Charon kills his future self. It doesn't stay dead for long: *"I told you that you couldn't kill me."*

In Hangar 18, K L'Hanna frees the Time Slider and they ride it out of time.

## VIII. The Kobrai Citadel

*"Okay, we've arrived. This is the legendary Kobrai Citadel."*

The Kobrai are the doolan's mythic "Architects of Reality", who weave existence with vast machines. A pra'min raid has torn open the **Protoverse**. Mindless, vicious **protomorphs** are pouring out, and stranded pra'min, abandoned by their mothership, roam the halls.

K L'Hanna is a **Kobrai Neophyte**, one of four guarding the Protoverse. The other three are scattered:
- one behind a smashed door that needs a toolkit;
- one whose crystal lies at the bottom of a reservoir;
- one who is simply trapped.

Each holds a crystal shard of a holographic key. Charon gathers all four and opens the heart of the Protoverse, where an artefact waits: **the Tyros Cantata**, the key to ultimate power.

## IX. The target

Then the neophyte tells him who his target is.

*"You have been here before, Charon. You don't remember, I know. But you stood just there holding the Tyros Cantata and you used it yourself. It turned you into Archon, the Dark God. It is him you must destroy."*

*"We Kobrai turned back time to give you a second chance… We took your memories, so the killing, the death, the pain and suffering you have seen in your life would not affect your decision today."*

*"Its power is absolute and it will corrupt you absolutely. Bring it to me. If you take it to Archon it will mean the end of existence."*

The Perfect Assassin holds the power of the universe in his hands, and his last target is the man he would become.

**Choose your fate!**

---

*The end of the script leaves the choice open: give the Cantata to the Kobrai, or keep it.*
